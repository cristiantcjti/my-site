I decide to create this blog to share real life experices of a software engineer in a world where it seems there is no more reality, where everyone seems perfect, super efficient with no flaws building rocket-science projects. 

However, the truth is a way from that and software engineering is more like an empty canvas where engineers work as artists, painting the next portrait filed with uncertaints but doing their best to make the least wrong decisions...

Unfortunately though, the IT industry is more like a jungle where we need to survive. It is at times  exciting, at times overwhelming to be out there fighting for our survival. It is like a maze that changes its routes everyday forcing us constantly to learn its patterns.

Have **you** ever felt like that?
