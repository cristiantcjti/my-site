## Is It Real? ##
You may have asked this question today, haven't you? I would guess that yes in case you watched some 'perfect' videos on the social medial you like the most. Well, if you asked yourself, I do not blame you. Specially with AI now, very likely questioning what is real will be around for a long time. 

This is one of the reasons why I decided to create this blog, to share real life experiences of a software engineer in a world where it seems there is no more reality, where everyone seems perfect, super efficient with no flaws building rocket-science projects. 

## The 'Sad' Reality ##
However, the truth is far from that where the real life of a software engineering is more like an empty canvas where engineers work as artists, working on the next painting filled with uncertainties but doing their best to make the least wrong decisions...

Unfortunately, the IT industry is more like a jungle where we need to survive. It is at times  exciting, at times overwhelming to be out there fighting for our survival. It is like a maze that changes its routes every day forcing us constantly to learn new patterns.

Sounds negative? It can be.... but at the same time, aren't these challenges that boost us as engineers? Isn't it true that each challenge overcome and the lessons learnt in the process fills us with dopamine, bringing feelings of pleasure, motivation, even happiness? And for me, this should be the main focus of our energy.

The feelings we have when we see the application or the solution we built running smoothly solving a real problem can be rewarding, and you know what? The words "running smoothly" are different from "running perfectly" which is the key point here. Countless people have been selling the idea of perfection in the IT industry resulting in a generation of engineers looking for such perfection in the code they write, in the solutions they bring, in the ideas they have or in the company they work for. Sad though, this thirsty for "perfection" has escalated to our lives, causing us to be sick for it. 

## Be Better not Perfect ##
It may sound hard to hear but yeah __"THERE IS NO PERFECT CODE"__, __"THERE IS NO PERFECT PROJECT"__, __"THERE IS NO PERFECT COMPANY"__.... and for now, it is better to accept it. Most of what is shown in social media, videos, companies' advertisements are not real. We are humans, we are imperfect and make mistakes and it doesn't mean we should passively accept it, rather it can mean we understand we and everyone else have flaws. For now, the treasure lives in trying to be better and not perfect.
